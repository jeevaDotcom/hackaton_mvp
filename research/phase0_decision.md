1. **Recommended primary dataset:** UCI Chronic Kidney Disease (DOI 10.24432/C5G020), 400 patient rows, 24 source predictors, 250 CKD/150 not-CKD, CC BY 4.0, verified source Apollo Hospitals, Managiri, Madurai Main Road, Karaikudi, Tamil Nadu, India.
2. **Recommended secondary validation datasets:** UCI Cleveland Heart Disease for compact cross-disease protocol replication; conditional West Bengal non-obese T2DM community cohort (Mendeley DOI 10.17632/c2mcxfw5m5.2) for Indian population relevance after schema audit. Pima is contextual only and is from Arizona, USA—not India.
3. **Strongest research gap found:** Leakage-free feature-budget evaluation combined with fold-wise selection-frequency/rank stability and paired classical/quantum models on identical CKD variables, transforms and folds.
4. **Second-best research gap:** Matched-dimension comparison of clinically interpretable selected variables versus PCA latent components for RBF-SVM and QSVC at 8, 6 and 4 dimensions.
5. **Third-best research gap:** Resource-aware utility profiles for only the finalist reduced circuits under ideal, 1,024/4,096-shot and explicitly labelled approximate-noise simulation, without a weighted “advantage” score.
6. **What previous studies already achieved:** Exact UCI CKD studies already report QSVM/QSVC, fidelity/angle/Pauli kernels, VQC/QNN/HQNN, top-eight and LASSO-13 feature selection, PCA/SVD, synthetic-noise tests, shot/design-space sweeps and up to 99–100% accuracy under heterogeneous protocols; [one direct PCA study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.infoh.2025.08.003) found classical SVM more accurate and 11.64× faster than QSVM.
7. **What exactly we will do differently:** Fit every imputer, encoder, scaler, selector, PCA transform, threshold and tuner inside training folds; use a pre-registered 24→12→8→6→4 source-variable ladder; pair SVM/QSVC on identical matrices; quantify stability and uncertainty; publish circuit/kernel/runtime resources; welcome a negative quantum result.
8. **The measurable hypothesis:** At least one fold-internally selected 8- or 6-variable signature will contain a core with selection frequency ≥0.80 and retain sensitivity and ROC-AUC within 0.05 of its full-feature classical reference; QSVC is exploratory non-inferior only if the lower paired 95% confidence bounds for QSVC−RBF-SVM exceed −0.05 for both sensitivity and ROC-AUC.
9. **Biggest scientific risk:** The 400-row, single-site, old and missing-heavy dataset may be so separable or workflow-specific that optimistic scores and stable variables do not generalise; an 80-row locked test cannot establish clinical non-inferiority.
10. **Biggest implementation risk:** Nested quantum-kernel evaluation scales quadratically in samples and multiplicatively across folds, budgets, feature maps and tuning, making 8–12-qubit and finite-shot/noise configurations exceed the 24–36 hour compute budget.
11. **Minimum viable experiment:** Audit/freeze UCI CKD; run full-24 LR/RF/XGBoost/RBF-SVM; nested MI/permutation selection at 8/6/4; paired ideal-statevector RBF-SVM versus ZZ-reps-1 QSVC on selected and, resources permitting, PCA inputs; report stability, primary metrics, paired confidence intervals, runtime/resources and one locked-test evaluation per frozen finalist.
12. **Stretch experiment:** Add shallow 4/6-qubit VQC with three starts, finalist 1,024/4,096-shot and N0/N1/N2 Aer simulation, then replicate the protocol on Cleveland and audit/run the West Bengal T2DM cohort without pooling disease outcomes.
13. **Which existing MVP components survive:** The visual shell, research disclaimer, feature-budget ladder as an unanswered question, artifact-backed classical/quantum comparison, actual-circuit/resource visualisation and a multi-axis utility profile.
14. **Which existing MVP components must change:** Disable/remove heuristic patient risk percentages and the Risk Cascade; remove the scalar Quantum Evidence Score, illustrative metrics/noise values, six-feature/75%/test-or-cost-saving claims and “Apollo, Chennai”; replace them only with versioned artifacts and verified Karaikudi provenance after Phase 0 review.
15. **GO / MODIFY / ABANDON recommendation:** **MODIFY**—go forward with CKD as a rigorous stability-aware feature-budget benchmark, but abandon the assumed six-feature compressor, clinical-test savings and quantum-advantage framing unless the pre-registered evidence later supports narrower claims.
