# Phase 0 references

Research cut-off: 24 August 2026. Links were checked against original repositories, publisher pages, full papers, or official documentation where available. A benchmark value is not transferable across rows unless the protocol and the `Comparability` field in `benchmark_matrix.md` support that comparison.

## Datasets and provenance

1. **UCI Chronic Kidney Disease.** Rubini, Soundarapandian & Eswaran (2015). 400 records, 24 predictors, CC BY 4.0. DOI [10.24432/C5G020](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5G020); [repository record](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/336/chronic+kidney+disease). The archive's own `chronic_kidney_disease.info.txt` identifies Dr P. Soundarapandian, Apollo Hospitals, Managiri, Madurai Main Road, Karaikudi, Tamil Nadu, India.
2. **UCI Heart Disease.** Janosi, Steinbrunn, Pfisterer & Detrano (1989). DOI [10.24432/C52P4X](https://doi.org/10.24432/C52P4X); [repository record](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/45/heart+disease). The processed Cleveland subset has 303 records and 13 predictors and is CC BY 4.0.
3. Detrano et al. (1989), “International application of a new probability algorithm for the diagnosis of coronary artery disease,” *American Journal of Cardiology*, 64, 304–310. DOI [10.1016/0002-9149(89)90524-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/0002-9149(89)90524-9).
4. Smith et al. (1988), “Using the ADAP Learning Algorithm to Forecast the Onset of Diabetes Mellitus.” [PMC2245318](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2245318/). This is the source study for the 768-row Pima dataset and explicitly locates the population near Phoenix, Arizona, USA.
5. Sarkar et al., **Non-obese T2DM Machine Learning Analysis**, Mendeley Data v2. DOI [10.17632/c2mcxfw5m5.2](https://doi.org/10.17632/c2mcxfw5m5.2), CC BY 4.0. Includes downloadable processed NFHS-4, CAB 2014 and a 714-subject West Bengal community cohort.
6. Sarkar et al. (2019), “Impaired compensatory hyperinsulinemia among nonobese type 2 diabetes patients: a cross-sectional study.” DOI [10.1177/2042018819889024](https://doi.org/10.1177/2042018819889024); [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6887811/).
7. Doppala & Bhattacharyya (2021), **Cardiovascular_Disease_Dataset**, Mendeley Data. DOI [10.17632/dzz48mvjht.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/dzz48mvjht.1), CC BY 4.0.
8. Doppala et al. (2022), “A Reliable Machine Intelligence Model for Accurate Identification of Cardiovascular Diseases Using Ensemble Techniques.” DOI [10.1155/2022/2585235](https://doi.org/10.1155/2022/2585235).
9. Kumar (2021), “Machine learning–based heart disease prediction system for Indian population: An exploratory study done in South India.” DOI [10.1016/j.mjafi.2020.10.013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mjafi.2020.10.013); [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8282535/). ## Methods and reporting

25. Nogueira, Sechidis & Brown (2018), “On the Stability of Feature Selection Algorithms,” *Journal of Machine Learning Research* 18(174), 1–54. [JMLR article and PDF](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v18/17-514.html). The protocol also reports transparent pairwise Jaccard and Kuncheva measures rather than relying on one statistic.

Qiskit Machine Learning 0.9.1, [FidelityQuantumKernel](https://qiskit-community.github.io/qiskit-machine-learning/stubs/qiskit_machine_learning.kernels.FidelityQuantumKernel.html) and [QSVC](https://qiskit-community.github.io/qiskit-machine-learning/stubs/qiskit_machine_learning.algorithms.QSVC.html) official API documentation.

Qiskit Aer 0.17.1, [building noise models](https://qiskit.github.io/qiskit-aer/tutorials/3_building_noise_models.html) and [device-noise simulation](https://qiskit.github.io/qiskit-aer/tutorials/2_device_noise_simulation.html). Aer documents depolarizing, thermal-relaxation and readout errors and warns that backend-derived noise models remain approximations.

## Verification notes

- `UNVERIFIED` in the research files means the reviewed primary/repository source did not disclose the item—not that the item is necessarily absent.
- `NR` means the cited study did not report the requested metric or configuration in the reviewed text/table.
- Common Pima CSV mirrors do not provide a single clean, authoritative licence chain comparable with current UCI CC BY records. The scientific source is traceable, but reuse terms for a chosen mirror must be verified before redistribution.
