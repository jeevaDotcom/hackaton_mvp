# Novelty Statement

Q-CARE’s novelty is an integrated evidence methodology for biomedical quantum ML, not a new quantum algorithm. It combines leakage-free stable feature selection, identical information budgets for classical RBF SVM and fidelity-kernel QSVC, paired benchmarking, PCA controls, and transparent runtime measurement. The same frozen pipeline is tested under missingness, measurement perturbation, reduced training size, finite-shot estimation, simulated noise, a cross-cohort kidney-disease stress test, and cross-disease validation. Q-CARE distinguishes internal feature stability from external feature transportability: in this experiment, a highly stable internal feature signature did not retain its discriminative relationships across cohorts. An artifact-backed claim registry and evidence dashboard keep supported, inconclusive, and negative outcomes visible together without presenting this result as a universal law.
