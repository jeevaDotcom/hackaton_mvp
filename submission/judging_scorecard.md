## Recommended judging posture

Lead with the strongest tension, not the technology: **internal RBF/QSVC AUCs of 1.000/0.999 became BD-KDD AUCs of 0.511/0.525, with both external intervals spanning chance**. Then show PARTIAL target comparability, identical-budget testing, and transparent runtime.
