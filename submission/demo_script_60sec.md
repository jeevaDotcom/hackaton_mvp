# 60-Second Emergency Demo — Final UI

## 0–15 seconds — Hook

**Action:** **Overview**, top.

“A 0.999 internal ROC-AUC sounds excellent. Q-CARE asks whether that quantum result survives fair comparison, compute cost, and a new cohort.”

## 15–30 seconds — Fair benchmark

**Action:** Click **CKD Benchmark**; use the rehearsed **Classical vs quantum** position.

“With the same eight variables and folds, QSVC sensitivity was 0.985 versus 1.000 for RBF SVM. It stayed internally competitive—but exact statevector QSVC was about 464 times slower.”

## 30–48 seconds — Reveal

**Action:** Click **Robustness & Shift**, top.

“On the BD-KDD stress test, RBF SVM AUC was 0.511 and QSVC 0.525; both confidence intervals included chance. Target comparability was only partial, and all eight feature-label signals flattened.”

## 48–60 seconds — Verdict

**Action:** Click **Quantum Evidence**, press **End**.

“Q-CARE does not assume quantum is better. It shows where quantum merits further study—and where the evidence says stop.”
